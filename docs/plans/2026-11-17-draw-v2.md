# /draw v2: draw on Claude's page, say what you mean, see the answer on the pad

Petros, 17 November 2026: "/draw needs serious improvements." This is the plan to design and build them. Not started.

## What went wrong on 17 November

Petros, on his phone, wanted to show Claude how Claude Voice's Sounds screen should look. Claude had just built an HTML
mock-up of it (https://fm.okimotus.com/claude-voice-sounds-screen-a2.html).

- The pad was blank. He drew the phone frame, three lanes and a card from scratch, and wrote "blah blah" in red for
  the text. He could not draw on top of the mock-up.
- Update sent one PNG with no words: the event text is a fixed `{type: "update"}` (`skills/draw/static/draw.js:75`).
  Claude guessed what each stroke meant.
- The PNG is cut to the area the strokes cover (`exportPng` and `bounds` in `skills/annotate/static/drawpad.js`).
- The pad never shows Claude's answer (`skills/draw/SKILL.md:19`). The rebuilt mock-up was at another link.
- The watch expires every 30 minutes and Claude re-arms it and says so (`SKILL.md:27-30`): noise.
- The pad has no Stop (`noCancel: true`, `draw.js:67`). He typed "stop the drawing" in the terminal.

## Build first

1. **Draw on top of Claude's page.** A new `draw.py show --image FILE [--text MSG]` puts an image on the pad as a
   background item. The pad draws it under the strokes, in the same coordinates, fitted to the view. A "Blank" toggle
   clears it. SKILL.md: run `show` right after building a mock-up. Image files first; a web page rendered to an image
   comes later.
2. **Send the whole picture.** With a background, Update sends the background with the strokes on top, uncropped, and
   the strokes alone on transparent, so Claude can tell Petros's marks from its own lines. `draw.py event` returns both.
3. **A note box beside Update** (16 px or larger, so the phone does not zoom; the keyboard's dictation works in it).
   Update sends `{type: "update", note}`, and a note with no strokes may be sent.
4. **Claude's answer on the pad.** `show --text` puts a short reply strip on the pad; a new `--image` replaces the
   background, so the rebuilt mock-up appears where he drew. The status reads "Claude is working" from Update until
   the reply comes.
5. **No 30-minute noise.** The watch re-arms silently; Claude speaks only on an Update, `DRAW_SUPERSEDED` or
   `DRAW_STOPPED`.
6. **A Stop button on the pad** (one confirm tap). It sends `{type: "stop"}`; the watch prints `DRAW_STOPPED`, ends and
   frees the pad; the drawing stays saved. `draw.py stop` does the same from the terminal.

Items 1, 2 and 4 share `show` and the background layer: build them together. 3, 5 and 6 stand alone.

## Later

Honest status ("Queued" when no session listens, instead of "Sent"); `event` acks by itself; a text tool; arrow and
box tools; a label naming the session that holds the pad.

## Out of scope

Several pages per drawing; a web page as background rendered automatically (after image backgrounds work).

## Open for design with Petros

- Where the note box and Stop sit on a phone screen, next to the tools and Update.
- How the reply strip looks and when it hides.
- Whether Claude's image replaces Petros's strokes or keeps them on top.
