Twelve ideas went to the committee of three and all twelve were built

Round 1, all 3 of 3 build
  • Table board redesign. Fixed full grid lit by colour and weight; column, rows and cell pairs; phones keep the whole grid.
  • Choose the board by the question. A decision table keyed on the question, table last. Fresh sessions picked a table in 7 of 13 answers before and 1 of 19 after.
  • Compare verdict strip. The outcome of a before/after is drawn and pointable. Key points beside the board were cut by amendment.
  • Quantity board (bars). Bars only, no series, thresholds or markers.
  • Structure board for commits and trees. Commit graphs and trees only.
  • Visual-first answers. Board opens on the first sentence, numbers in the note, zones for processes. Visual questions then drew: graph 4, bars 2, sequence 1, table 0 of 7.
Round 2, all 3 of 3 build
  • Name a part by its distinctive word, never by order. The picture no longer contradicts the voice; an unnamed part is shown with the rest, never lit by position.
  • Answer shape gate. Table only for 4x4 or smaller of 1-3 words, a no-board rule, two nudges. A nudge about board-after-N-sentences was dropped (1 of 3). Fresh sessions on table-prone questions: tables 8 of 8 before, 2 of 8 after.
  • Phone fit policy. Narrowed: graph turned for 390 px, map fits the width, 11 px text floor. Table card redesign and edge fade dropped.
  • Small table follows the voice. Unique-prefix naming allowed (2 of 3); an ambiguous name lights nothing.
  • Verdict closes every board and is pointable.
  • A refused board is never replaced silently. Narrowed: a bare code path is accepted (2 of 3); a render-failure post-back channel was dropped by vote.
