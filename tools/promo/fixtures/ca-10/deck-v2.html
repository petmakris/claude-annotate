Why shop-api drops 1 in 200 payments today, and the three changes that fix it.

01 · THE PROBLEM
Why checkout calls fail today
  • A timeout is not a failure.The card network answers in 9 s at peak, but shop-api gives up after 5 s and shows the customer an error page, even when the payment went through a moment later.
  • A blind retry can charge twice.The second call carries no idempotency key, so the bank cannot tell it apart from a brand new order and takes the money again for the same basket.
  • Nobody sees it happen.Failed checkouts are logged at debug level only. They never reach the dashboard, so the on-call engineer has no way to notice the rate going up after a release.
3
01 · THE PROBLEM
What it costs us
  • Support finds out first.Customers write in about a double charge two days later, when the statement arrives, and the ticket rarely names the order the charge belongs to.
  • Refunds cost more than the sale.Each double charge takes a manual refund, a card fee that is not returned, and an apology voucher that the finance team books by hand.
  • It gets worse every Friday.Peak traffic doubles the network delay, so the share of checkouts that time out climbs from 1 in 200 to about 1 in 80 between six and nine in the evening. That is also when the shop takes a third of its weekly orders, so the losses land on the busiest hours.
4
02 · THE CHANGE
Three changes, one release
  • Wait 12 s, not 5 s.Matches the slowest answer the card network gave in November.
  • Send one idempotency key per order.A retry reuses it, so the bank charges once.
  • Count every failed checkout.A dashboard tile, and an alert above 1 in 1,000.
