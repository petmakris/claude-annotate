A graph board shows what happens to commits or to a tree, before and after. [[point: before]] Before, main has a first commit and a second commit. Your first and your second commit branch off the second one, and someone else has added a new tip to main. [[point: after]] A rebase copies your commits onto the new tip. [[point: node d2]] Your first commit, copied, is a new commit with a new parent, and your second, copied, follows it. [[point: node d]] The originals are still there, faint, but nothing reaches them. [[point: both]] That is why a rebase rewrites history and a merge does not.
