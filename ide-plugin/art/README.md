# Side-bar icons

The 512 px masters of the two tool-window icons. The plugin ships
`src/main/resources/icons/toolwindow_review.png` and
`toolwindow_walkthrough.png`, 20 px with a 40 px `@2x` for Retina screens,
both made from these. The diff gutter keeps its own SVG markers.

Drawn on 10 November 2026 by Gemini (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`), in the
style of Claude Voice's command bar icons: two of them (the microphone and
the paper plane) went in as the style reference. The prompt asked for a
single object on solid magenta `#FF00FF`, and the magenta was cut out by
colour-to-alpha, not by a mint cutout, which leaves black edges on
white objects.

- `toolwindow_review.png`: a white speech bubble with a yellow pencil.
- `toolwindow_walkthrough.png`: a compass with a blue needle.
