01 · HOW IT WORKS
The browser reads, Claude writes

The page renders the deck as itself — every slide, in order, at the size it will be shown.

Clicking an element resolves it to a line range in the source file, not to a search string.

Your comment becomes one queued instruction; nothing else touches the file.

A comment is a request, not an edit. The tool never rewrites the document on your behalf — it hands Claude an address and waits.
  • Land the address point: line ranges survive entities, a text search does not.
  • If asked why one comment at a time — edits interact.
3
01 · WHAT IS ADDRESSABLE
Everything with a class, and every line inside it
  • Each direct child of a slide that carries a class becomes a target.
  • Each paragraph and list item inside one of those becomes its own target.
  • The gutter number is skipped, because the harness rewrites it at load.
  • Speaker notes are hidden in the slide, so they get a column of their own.
  • Four rules, no schema. Anything narrower needs markup nobody has written.
