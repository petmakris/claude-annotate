# Presentation style

The house style for every deck in this folder. The `slides` and `deck` skills read this
file before writing or changing a slide; edit it to make them write the way you present.

## Length

- One claim per slide, stated as the slide's title.
- At most four bullets. A fifth is a second slide.
- A bullet is a claim in bold plus at most two sentences of evidence.

## Words

- Say what is true, in plain sentences. No slogans, no rhetorical questions as titles.
- Numbers, names and dates beat adjectives.

## What goes on a slide

- What the room must read. What you will say anyway stays in `notes.md`.
- A live demo gets a slide that names what to watch for, not a transcript of the demo.
